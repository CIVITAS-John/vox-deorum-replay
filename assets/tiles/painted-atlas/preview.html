Painted atlas texture review

Source art for Stage 4. The sample below shows hex clipping, repeated tiles, feature combinations, and territory tint. It is an art preview, not the proposed renderer or a performance benchmark.

Relief uses one hill or mountain per tile. Oasis and marsh leave more ground visible, and flood plains add a light fertile accent. Coast uses a muted aquatic blue-green.

Below 10 px, the sample uses flat colors. All relief and features are visible from 10 px onward. Larger sizes preserve the same features with sharper painted shapes. Rivers, borders, labels, and transition hysteresis belong to renderer implementation.
